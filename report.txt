II. EXPERIMENT
================================================================
1. Implementation and the code, step by step, mapped to the maths
- Step A (BI profile):
  Code: hidden_states = model(**ids, output_hidden_states=True).hidden_states
        BI_l = 1 - cosine_similarity(h_l, h_{l+1}).mean()
  Maths: d_l = 1 - (h_l . h_{l+1}) / (||h_l|| ||h_{l+1}||), averaged over
  batch*sequence. Low d_l => layer l approximates identity.
- Step B (residual capture):
  Code: for each target L, register_forward_pre_hook on blocks[L] records X,
        register_forward_hook records Y.
  Maths: X = h_in^(L) (N x d), Y = h_out^(L); residual Delta = Y - X.
- Step C (bridge fit):
  Code: bridge = FittedTernaryBridge(d, rank=32); bridge.fit(X, Y) runs Adam
        on U,V to minimize MSE(X@U@V, Delta).
  Maths: min_{U,V} || X U V - Delta ||_F^2.
- Step D (ternary quantize):
  Code: gamma = mean(|W|); Wq = round(W/gamma).clamp(-1,1)*gamma.
  Maths: this is AbsMean from BitNet b1.58; Wq in {-gamma,0,gamma}.
- Step E (replace and evaluate):
  Code: blocks[L] = bridge.to(dtype); PPL via the same 256-token
        non-overlapping windows as Assignment 1.
  Maths: PPL = exp( (1/N) sum_i -log p_theta(x_i | x_<i) ).

2. Results
Fitted-ternary TerGRASP PPL by k (validation):
  GPT-2:          k=1 45.11, k=2 50.09, k=3 140.42, k=4 879.52
  SmolLM-135M:    k=1 21.71, k=2 40.49, k=3 33.05, k=4 38.49
Hard-removal control (Approach 06):
  GPT-2:          45.76 / 47.95 / 121.81 / 943.24
  SmolLM-135M:    19.93 / 26.13 / 29.91 / 34.20
k=1 matches or slightly beats removal; higher k tracks removal. The win is
not lower PPL but preserved PPL with a ternary, ~1.58-bit block.

3. Findings and patterns
- BI ranking works: targeted k>=2 removal beats random 3x-30x on PPL.
- k=1 is free for either method - one block is rarely critical.
- Affine/naive-merge/tying variants fail: layer maps are not affine and
  neurons are not aligned; a fitted low-rank bridge is the minimal fix.
- Low-BI layers tolerate 2-bit quantization with no PPL change, so BI
  predicts both removability and compressibility.

4. All 15 approaches, one-line summary
01 fitted ternary TerGRASP - works, headline
02 affine bridge across gap - fails (PPL 10^3x)
03 BI + KD repair - scaffold, KD loss ~0 at this budget
04 permutation-aligned averaging - fell back to naive, fails
05 LayerDrop->BI->SVD->KD pipeline - scaffold only
06 ShortGPT BI baseline - works, the control
07 SVD low-rank - works on SmolLM (r=256 ~= baseline)
08 weight merging - always worse
09 KD shallow student - partial recovery, budget too small
10 learned L0 gates - gates never close
11 LayerDrop - no separation between depths
12 attn/FFN proxy pruning - stub
